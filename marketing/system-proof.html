Why leakage stops

Four places revenue leaks — closed by design.

①

Invisible activity

If you can't see who's actually dialing, you can't fix it. BASIS counts 100% of calls per BDE — transcript or not — so effort is never guesswork.

②

“Connected” that isn’t

A long call to a voicemail isn’t a conversation. BASIS only credits a Right-Party Contact when a real decision-maker is reached.

③

Pitches that drift

The AI checks every transcript for the full pitch, genuine interest, and your qualification bar — and flags low-confidence calls for review.

④

Outcomes lost to admin

Booked meetings are detected from the call itself — no manual CRM entry — so won commitments are never dropped on the floor.

⑤

Blame without a cause

The leaderboard shows each rep’s stage-by-stage conversion, so coaching targets the exact leak instead of the loudest number.

⑥

Fresh vs Followup blur

Every stage splits new outreach from nurture, revealing whether the leak is in prospecting or in the follow-through.
